🧪 Test: Academic Year Display After Refresh

Step 1: Add a New Academic Year

  1. Go to FacultyAdmin.php
  2. Click "Academic Year" in the sidebar
  3. Click "Add New" button
  4. Fill in the form:
    • Academic Year: 2024-2025
    • Semester: 1st Semester
    • Start Date: Today's date
    • End Date: 1 month from today
  5. Click "Save"

Step 2: Verify Data is Displayed

You should see the new academic year in the table
Status should show "Inactive" button

Step 3: Refresh the Page (F5 or Ctrl+R)

Press F5 to refresh

Step 4: Go Back to Academic Year Section

  1. After refresh, you'll be on the Dashboard
  2. Click "Academic Year" in the sidebar again

✅ Expected Results:

  • Data should still be there! (2024-2025, 1st Semester)
  • The table should load automatically when you click Academic Year
  • Status button should show "Inactive"
  • You can click "Inactive" to activate it

Step 5: Test Activation

  1. Click the "Inactive" button
  2. It should change to "Active" (green)
  3. Refresh the page again (F5)
  4. Go back to Academic Year section
  5. The status should still show "Active"
